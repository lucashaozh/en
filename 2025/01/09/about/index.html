About me

My name is Shenghao Zhang (Lucas). I am currently a Master student in ETH Zurich majoring in Visual Computing. Before joining ETH, I completed my bachelor study as a first honor student in The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

My interested fields include Computer Graphics, especially real-time rendering and simulation, and Compute Vision. I am excited to explore the state-of-the-art technology in graphics and vision, and apply them to solve different kind of problems in the industry. I hope to create a realistic, immersive and interactive world with amazing algorithms one day in the future.
